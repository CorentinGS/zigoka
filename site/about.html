Zigoka

Play 9×9 · Play 19×19

Engine

Written in Zig, compiled to WebAssembly. Beginner plays the network's top move; the other levels add a PUCT tree search, which thinks for 0.3 s at Medium and 1 s at Strong. The 19×19 network is larger and the board has more points, so a search there sees fewer positions in the same time.

Network

9×9: Moka v2 by Million Software, MIT license (license, notices).

19×19: moka-19, which starts from the Moka v2 trunk and is distilled from the KataGo network kata1-b18c384nbt-s9996604416-d4316597426 (KataGo network license).

Rules

Simple ko, no suicide, area scoring, komi 7. Two passes end the game. The stones the engine judges dead are then marked; click a group to mark it dead or alive again, and accept the score once the marking is right. Undo returns to the game.

Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. No game data leaves your machine. Page views are counted by Cloudflare Web Analytics, without cookies.
