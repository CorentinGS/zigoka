Zigoka

Play

Engine

Written in Zig, compiled to WebAssembly. Beginner plays the network's top move; the other levels add a PUCT tree search.

Network

Moka 9×9 by Million Software, MIT license (license, notices).

Rules

Simple ko, no suicide, area scoring, komi 7. Two passes end the game; dead stones are removed before scoring.

Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. No game data leaves your machine. Page views are counted by Cloudflare Web Analytics, without cookies.
